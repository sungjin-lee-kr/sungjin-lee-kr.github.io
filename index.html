Climate Modeling & Atmospheric Science

I study how the climate system responds not only to the level of warming, but also to the pathway taken to reach it. My current research uses Earth system model experiments and emulator-based approaches to investigate surface ozone and East Asian precipitation under different CO₂ drawdown pathways.
